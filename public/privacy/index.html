Privacy Policy

Clueframe: Picture Riddles (Android, package name com.clueframe.puzzle)
Effective date: September 5, 2026

1. Overview

Clueframe Games (“we”, “us”, “our”) publishes the mobile game Clueframe: Picture Riddles (the “App”) and operates this website (the “Site”). This Privacy Policy explains what information is processed when you use the App or the Site, how it is used, with whom it is shared, and the choices you have. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the App.

The Site consists of static pages only: it sets no cookies and does not use analytics or tracking scripts.

2. Information We Collect

The App does not require an account or registration. We do not ask for, and do not knowingly collect, your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location through the App.

2.1 Information collected automatically by third-party SDKs

When you use the App, the advertising and analytics services integrated in it (listed in Sections 3 and 4) automatically collect:

  • device identifiers, including the Android Advertising ID and app-instance identifiers;
  • IP address and the approximate (city-level) location derived from it;
  • device and network information, such as device model, operating system version, system language, and mobile carrier or country;
  • usage data, such as screens viewed, levels played, session length, and interactions with ads;
  • diagnostic information about the performance of the App and of the ads shown in it.

2.2 Information stored only on your device

Your game progress, coin balance, hints, and settings (such as sound and language) are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to us. Clearing the App’s data or uninstalling the App removes them.

2.3 Information you give us directly

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and the contents of your message. We use them only to respond to you.

The exact set of networks serving ads can vary by region and App version. We update this list when the set of integrated networks changes.

3.1 Your advertising choices

  • Consent dialog. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and other regions where required by law, the App shows a consent dialog before personalized ads are served. You can decline personalized advertising and see non-personalized (contextual) ads instead.
  • Device settings. On Android you can delete or reset your Advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Withdrawing consent. You can change your consent choice at any time through the App’s consent dialog where available, or by contacting us (Section 13).

4. Analytics

We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the App is used in aggregate — for example, which levels are completed, which features are used, and how long players stay. Firebase Analytics processes device identifiers, IP address, and app-interaction events on our behalf.

5. How We Use Information

We and our service providers use the information described above to:

  • operate and provide the App;
  • serve and measure advertising, which funds the free App;
  • understand aggregate usage in order to improve the App;
  • maintain security and prevent fraud and abuse;
  • comply with legal obligations.

Where the GDPR or similar laws apply, our legal bases are: your consent (for personalized advertising — you can withdraw it at any time); our legitimate interests (serving non-personalized ads, analytics, security, and fraud prevention); and compliance with legal obligations.

6. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. Information is shared only:

  • with the advertising and analytics providers listed in Sections 3 and 4, which process it under their own privacy policies;
  • with public authorities where disclosure is required by applicable law or a valid legal request;
  • with a successor entity in the event of a merger, acquisition, or similar transaction, in which case this policy will continue to apply or you will be notified of changes.

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), the use of advertising identifiers for cross-context personalized advertising may be considered “sharing”. You can opt out through the consent dialog, your device’s ads settings (Section 3.1), or by contacting us.

7. Data Retention

  • We do not maintain our own servers or databases of personal information.
  • Firebase Analytics retains user-level data for up to 14 months.
  • Advertising partners retain data according to their own published policies.
  • Data stored locally on your device (Section 2.2) remains until you clear the App’s data or uninstall it.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information; to restrict or object to its processing; to withdraw consent at any time (without affecting prior processing); and to not be discriminated against for exercising these rights. California residents may also request details about the categories of information collected and shared.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us (Section 13). We may need to verify your request. Because most data described in this policy is held by the SDK providers rather than by us, we may also direct you to the relevant provider’s own privacy controls. If you are in the EEA or the UK, you additionally have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

9. Children’s Privacy

The App is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13 (or under the applicable age of digital consent in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us and we will take steps to have it deleted.

10. Security

11. International Data Transfers

The providers listed in this policy may process data on servers located in various countries, including the United States. Where required, they rely on appropriate safeguards for such transfers, such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses.
